## What's New in v0.2.0

### Highlights
- **Sound Lab Real-Time Audio DSP (`Ctrl+E`)** — Zero-dependency pure-Rust 5-band Parametric Equalizer (`Flat`, `Bass Boost`, `Late Night`, `Vocal`, `Electronic`, `Lo-Fi`, plus custom vertical sliders), Studio Stereo Reverb, Playback Speed (`0.75x`–`1.50x`), 1-click **Slowed + Reverb** (`Ctrl+Shift+S`), **Nightcore**, and a smooth-fading **Sleep Timer**.
- **GPU Canvas Spectrum Visualizers & Reactive Ambient Mesh** — Real-time 512-point Cooley-Tukey FFT powering **Bars**, **Wave**, and **Halo** GPU visualizers, a live Player Bar **MiniMeter**, and a bass-reactive floating obsidian ambient backdrop (`Ctrl+Shift+V`).
- **Zen Cinema Mode (`Ctrl+Shift+Z`)** — Ultra-clean distraction-free fullscreen stage with glowing album art, live spectrum visualizer, and large clickable synced lyrics.
- **Endless Auto-Radio & Queue Reordering** — Smart Radio seamlessly seeds matching tracks when you play a single song or reach the end of your queue, plus `↑` / `↓` queue reordering and a 1-click **Radio** button.
- **Spotlight Command Palette (`Ctrl+K`) & Upgraded Home** — Instant keyboard-first launcher for every control, EQ preset, vibe, and search, plus quick Home Vibe chips, a frosted **Jump Back In** hero card, and floating toast notifications.
