A fast, native desktop client for YouTube Music.

hum is an open-source desktop application built in Rust. Instead of wrapping a heavy web browser, it renders a GPU-accelerated native interface and decodes audio natively with minimal memory and CPU overhead.

Browse & Search

Explore Home feeds, curated mixes, Moods & Genres, New Releases, and search across songs and playlists.

Native Playback

Buffered audio decoding with shuffle, repeat modes, queue management, seeking, and volume controls.

Optional Google Sync

Sign in via Google OAuth 2.0 to view your YouTube playlists and liked songs directly inside the app. Tokens stay strictly local on your device.
